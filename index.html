“Move identities, not only mail.”

Mailshift design principle

A migration you can audit

Local data. Human-controlled changes. Explicit proof.

  1. 1

    Import domains only

    Use a credential-free CSV. Password, username, email, OTP, secret, and recovery-code columns are rejected.

  2. 2

    Work from highest risk

    Identity and recovery roots rise above shopping, social, and entertainment accounts.

  3. 3

    Follow source-linked guidance

    Reviewed playbooks link to provider settings and official help. Unreviewed domains stay clearly labeled.

  4. 4

    Prove the handoff

    Track address change, recovery update, verification, fresh login, and old-address cleanup separately.
